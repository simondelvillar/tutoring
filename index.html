About me

Hi, I’m Simon. I tutor math and physics for high school students and for first-year university students at UBC Okanagan.

Most students who come to me aren’t bad at math or physics. They’re missing one idea that everything after it builds on. In our sessions we find that idea, work through it together, and practice until you can do it without me.

I care about building confidence, not just getting answers, so expect me to ask you more questions than I hand you solutions.

Booking & Cancellation Policy

A few simple rules so sessions run smoothly for both of us.

  • 💳

    Payment: Paid sessions are paid when you book. The free 15-minute chat is always free.

  • 🔁

    Rescheduling: Free with at least 24 hours’ notice. Use the link in your confirmation email.

  • ❌

    Late cancellations: Cancelling with less than 24 hours’ notice means the session is forfeited, unless it’s a genuine emergency, in which case just message me.

  • ⏰

    Running late: I’ll wait up to 10 minutes. Your session still ends at the scheduled time.

  • 👻

    No-shows: If you don’t show up and don’t message me, the session is forfeited.

  • 🙋

    If I need to cancel: You’ll get a full refund or a free reschedule, your choice.

  • 💻

    What you’ll need: A quiet spot, a stable internet connection, and whatever you’re working on (textbook, notes, or problem set).

How we’ll work

  1. Free 15-minute chat

    Fifteen minutes to find where you’re stuck and see if we click.

  2. A plan for your goals

    An exam, a grade, or a course you’re behind in. We set targets and a weekly schedule.

  3. Weekly sessions

    Online or in person, with practice problems between sessions and a short progress note.
